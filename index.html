UNDERSTANDING ENDOMETRIOSIS

Start by understanding your experience.

Endometriosis is more than period pain. It can affect physical health, emotional wellbeing, relationships, work and everyday life.

♡

What is Endometriosis?

Endometriosis is a chronic condition in which tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside the uterus. It can cause pain and other symptoms and may affect different parts of the body.

Learn more →
+

Common Symptoms

Symptoms can vary from person to person. They may include severe menstrual pain, pelvic pain, pain during sex, fatigue and bowel or bladder symptoms.
