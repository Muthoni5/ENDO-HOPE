UNDERSTANDING DIAGNOSIS

Getting a Diagnosis

Understanding what happens during the diagnostic journey can help you prepare for conversations with healthcare professionals.

Why can diagnosis be difficult?

Endometriosis can be difficult to identify because symptoms can vary widely and may overlap with symptoms of other conditions.

Some people may also experience symptoms for a long time before receiving an explanation for their pain or other difficulties.

Your pain is worth discussing.

Persistent or severe symptoms that affect your daily life deserve to be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

What may happen during the diagnostic process?

There is no single experience that applies to everyone. A healthcare professional may consider several pieces of information when evaluating symptoms.

01

Your medical history

You may be asked about your menstrual cycle, pain, symptoms, previous treatments and how your symptoms affect everyday life.

02

Physical examination

Depending on your symptoms and clinical situation, a healthcare professional may recommend a physical examination.

03

Imaging and other tests

Your healthcare professional may recommend investigations such as ultrasound or other imaging depending on your circumstances.

04

Clinical assessment

Your symptoms, medical history, examination and investigations may be considered together when deciding what care or further evaluation is appropriate.

Preparing for your appointment

You do not need to remember everything during an appointment. Writing down important information beforehand can make the conversation easier.

What symptoms do I have?

Write down the symptoms you experience and when they usually occur.

How long have I experienced them?

Note when you first noticed the symptoms and whether they have changed over time.

How does it affect my life?

Consider work, school, relationships, sleep and other everyday activities.

What have I already tried?

Make a note of previous treatments, medications or healthcare visits.

Questions you can ask

01 What could be causing my symptoms?

02 What investigations might be appropriate?

03 What treatment options are available?

04 What should I do if my symptoms continue?

05 When should I come back for follow-up?

You deserve to be heard.

If your symptoms are affecting your quality of life, consider seeking professional medical evaluation and explaining clearly what you have been experiencing.
