About this fundraiser

Right now, contributions made here go directly toward the ENDO HOPE founder's own endometriosis surgery. Living with endometriosis is what inspired ENDO HOPE, and this fundraiser is a personal step in that same journey.

As ENDO HOPE grows into a fully registered organization with its own programs, future contributions will be clearly separated from personal medical costs. Until then, we wanted to be upfront about exactly where your contribution goes.

Why this matters

Your support of this surgery directly helps build ENDO HOPE, which is working toward:

  • 💜 Support women living with endometriosis
  • 🩺 Connect women with reliable medical information and specialists
  • 🎓 Create endometriosis awareness and education
  • 🤝 Build peer-support communities
  • 🏥 Work toward helping women access diagnosis and treatment
  • 📢 Advocate for better understanding of endometriosis in Kenya
