Share Your Story

Your experience may help another woman feel less alone in hers.

Your story matters.

Endometriosis can be an isolating experience. Sometimes hearing from someone who has walked a similar path can make a difference.

You can share your experience with diagnosis, treatment, pain, healthcare, relationships, work, school or simply what living with endometriosis has been like for you.

Before you share

Please do not include passwords, identification numbers, medical records, financial information or other information that could put your privacy at risk.

Maximum 3,000 characters.

Privacy matters.

ENDO HOPE will not publish a submitted story automatically. Stories should be reviewed before publication, and we will establish clear privacy and consent procedures before accepting real submissions.
