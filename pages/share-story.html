ENDO WARRIORS

Share Your Story

Your experience may help another woman feel less alone in hers.

Your story matters.

Endometriosis can be an isolating experience. Sometimes hearing from someone who has walked a similar path can make a difference.

You can share your experience with diagnosis, treatment, pain, healthcare, relationships, work, school or simply what living with endometriosis has been like for you.

What can you share?

Your journey is your own. You can talk about your symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, challenges accessing care, relationships, everyday life, or something you wish you had known earlier.

You can also share a message you would like another woman living with endometriosis to hear.

Before you share

You can choose to share your story anonymously, use your first name, or use another name or nickname.

Please only provide information that you are comfortable sharing. Do not include passwords, identification numbers, financial information, medical records or other sensitive information that could put your privacy at risk.

Ready to share your story?

Complete the ENDO HOPE story submission form. Your submission will be reviewed before it is considered for publication on the ENDO HOPE website.

Submitting a story does not automatically mean that it will be published. You will be able to tell us how you would like your story to appear and whether you are comfortable being contacted about your submission.

Share Your Story
Your privacy matters.

ENDO HOPE does not publish submitted stories automatically. Stories are reviewed before publication, and contributors can choose how their name or identity is presented.

By submitting your story, you are giving ENDO HOPE an opportunity to review your experience for possible publication as part of the ENDO WARRIORS community.
