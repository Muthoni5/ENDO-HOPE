SUPPORT ENDO HOPE

Help us create a community where no woman feels alone.

Your support can help us provide information, connect Endo Warriors with resources and create a stronger community for women in Kenya.

What does your support help us do?

ENDO HOPE is being built around a simple idea: women living with endometriosis deserve to be heard, informed and supported.

📚

Education

Help us create accessible information about endometriosis, symptoms, diagnosis, treatment and navigating care.

🤝

Community

Help us create spaces where Endo Warriors can share experiences and know that they are not alone.

🏥

Access to Care

Help us build and maintain a trusted directory of healthcare and support resources across Kenya.

📣

Awareness

Help us raise awareness about the realities women face when seeking diagnosis and care.
