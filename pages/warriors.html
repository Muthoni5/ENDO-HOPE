ENDO WARRIORS

Real stories. Real journeys. You are not alone.

Behind every diagnosis is a story. Endo Warriors is a space for women to share their experiences, learn from one another and find strength in community.

OUR STORIES

Every Endo Warrior has a story.

Endometriosis can affect women in deeply different ways. Some stories begin with years of unexplained pain. Others begin with a diagnosis, a difficult journey through care, or finally finding people who understand.

These stories remind us that no two journeys are exactly the same — but no woman should have to feel alone in hers.

🌿

WARRIOR STORY 01

"The Pain Nobody Could See"

For a long time, I became very good at pretending I was okay. People saw a woman who was functioning. They didn't see everything it took to get through the day.

WARRIOR STORY 02

"My Journey: From Period Pain to an Endometriosis Diagnosis."

My endometriosis journey began when I was 13 years old and started experiencing period pain. Years later, I received a diagnosis and continued navigating life with endometriosis.

🌸

WARRIOR STORY 03

"I learned to speak up for myself."

There was a time when I stopped asking questions because I was afraid of being told that nothing was wrong. Eventually, I realised that I deserved answers.
