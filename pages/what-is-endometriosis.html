UNDERSTANDING ENDOMETRIOSIS

What is Endometriosis?

Understanding the condition is an important first step toward understanding your experience and seeking appropriate care.

What is endometriosis?

Endometriosis is a chronic condition in which tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside the uterus.

This tissue can respond to hormonal changes during the menstrual cycle. For some people, this can contribute to inflammation, pain and other symptoms.

Endometriosis affects people differently.

The severity of symptoms does not always correspond to the extent of disease, and experiencing significant pain is a reason to seek appropriate medical evaluation.

Where can it affect the body?

Endometriosis commonly involves areas within the pelvis, although it can affect other areas of the body as well.

Symptoms may depend on where endometriosis is located and how it affects surrounding tissues.

Why does it matter?

Endometriosis can affect more than physical health. Living with persistent symptoms can influence work, education, relationships, emotional wellbeing and everyday activities.

What should I do if I think I may have it?

If you are experiencing symptoms that concern you, consider discussing them with a qualified healthcare professional.

Keeping track of your symptoms, their timing, severity and impact on your daily life can help you communicate your experience during a healthcare appointment.

Want to prepare for a healthcare visit?

ENDO HOPE is developing resources to help you organize your symptoms and questions before an appointment.
